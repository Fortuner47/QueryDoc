Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is one of the greatest goalscorers in football history, but he is also one of the most polarizing players among fans.

Critics often label him a "penalty merchant" because a significant number of his career goals have come from the penalty spot. They argue that he actively seeks penalties and benefits from being the designated penalty taker for nearly every team he has played for.

Common criticisms include:
- Scores many penalties.
- Prioritizes personal goal records.
- Takes free kicks despite a relatively low conversion rate.
- Sometimes appears frustrated when teammates score instead of him.

Supporters counter these claims by pointing out that:
- Winning and converting penalties is a skill.
- He has scored over 900 career goals from open play, headers, free kicks, and penalties combined.
- He is one of the best aerial finishers in football history.
- His longevity and consistency across multiple leagues are unmatched.

The nickname "penalty merchant" is mostly used as football banter by rival fans rather than as a serious football analysis.
